In 147 Post Sociatem, a horrific fate befalls on the students of Hevrion Adventuring Society (HAS), where its members massacred in the Maledictus Forest, leaving only a survivor. Two years later, the Principal of Hevrion Magic Campus, Leonidas, gathered some of the most talented freshmen to rebuild HAS.

Thus, the new HAS shall explore and unravel Vrey’s secrets, whilst preparing of what is to come.
