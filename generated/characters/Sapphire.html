Sapphire, real name Minerva Dale, is a Professor of Magical Engineering employed by HMC. She is a Firbolg

Title

  • Professor of Hevrion Magic Campus

Appearance

She wears a purple HMC robe with gold patterns resembling a sine wave.

Backstory

'To be discovered'

Personality & Traits

Sapphire is brilliant and experimentally driven, with a particular expertise in magical items. She loves to experiment but has a deep aversion to paperwork, which is why she rejected the position of Leonidas' assistant despite her capabilities. Those close to her call her "Sapphy."

Relationships

  • Leonidas of Hevrion: Leonidas and Sapphire are colleagues at HMC. She rejected his offer to become his assistant, which ultimately led to Mira taking the position instead.

  • Mira: Co-discovered the properties of Alkahest together, studying the liquid found in a device that Leiden used to carry.

  • Emilia of Vrey: Emilia held Sapphire with warm personal regard, but the latter personally felt Emilia somewhat as a nuisance due to her explosive hard-to-clean magic engineering experiments.
