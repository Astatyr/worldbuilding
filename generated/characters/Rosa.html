Rosa is the daughter of Count Sylbil. She is a halfling.

Title

  • Rosette Star

  • ‘Fate is unraveling…’

Appearance

Rosa Vrey stands at 140 cm. She has short, disheveled crimson hair and pointed nose. She has expressive brown eyes but is often unfocused. Before the Lugdunum Ballroom Assassination attempt, she wears an Earpiece that feeds her Nectos’ Prescripts.

After the incident. Her left ear has missing sections and burns scars due to the exploded earpiece. She wears a silk, maroon glove on her left hand to hide her Monoceros constellation mark.

Her HMC robe has a gold embroidery of the Monoceros constellation and a red rose motif.

Backstory

‘To be discovered’

Personality & Traits

She often stutters and avoids eye-contact. She gains a little bit of confidence and much more calm after the Lugdunum Ballroom Assassination attempt

During Willbearer state, the constellation mark emits fierce red aura that envelops her and greatly enhances her physical capabilities.

Relationships

  • Pierre: Pierre saved Rosa from an owlbear attack on the summit of Mount Hara that killed her father. He brought Rosa into HMC, where he gave her a modified HAS earpiece that feeds her with Prescripts.
