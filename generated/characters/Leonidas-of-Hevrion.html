Leonidas of Hevrion, also known as Leonidas Gael H. IX, is the principal of HMC from 135 Post Sociatem. He is a half-elf half-human.

Title

  • Principal of Hevrion Magic Campus

  • Blue Comet

Appearance

Leonidas is 173 cm tall. He has short, messy white hair with a faint yellow tinge at its ends. He wears a purple HMC robe interweaved with gold and white patterns depicting mountains and a fallen star.

Backstory

‘To be discovered’

Personality & Traits

Composed and measured even under pressure. Seemingly cannot leave the grounds of Hevrion Magic Campus. Directly supervises HAS.

Relationships

  • Heinrich II of Vrey: Leonidas' younger brother, the late Emperor of Vrey. Leonidas abdicated his position as crown prince, passing the title to Heinrich.

  • Emilia of Vrey: His niece. They hold private tea parties from time to time to discuss matters of all kinds.

  • Lily: Lily and Leonidas enter HMC as a student at the same time, become rivals, close friends, alumnus, and then research colleagues together before Lily left HMC at 133 Post Sociatem. The nature of their connection is known only to those closest to him.
