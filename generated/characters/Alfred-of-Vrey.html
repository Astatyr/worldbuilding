Alfred of Vrey is the Crown Prince of Vrey Empire during the reign of Emperor Heinrich II of Vrey and succeeds Heinrich II after his death. He is exceptionally tall for half-dwarf half-human.

Title

  • Prince Alfred of Vrey (150 Post Sociatem)

  • Emperor Alfred of Vrey

  • The Little Emperor

Appearance

Alfred stands at 160 cm. He has short blonde hair and royal blue eyes. He has trimmed moustache, and his hands are slightly calloused from fencing. He always brings a small notebook.

In formal settings he wears white and golden court attire, but sometimes wears Velvet blue for Hevrion related events, reflecting his alumnus status. He carried a simple wand and wears a ring with Protection wards at each of his fingers.

Backstory

‘To be discovered’

Personality & Traits

He is meticulous and calm, but sometimes timid when under pressure.

He excels in movement and defensive magic.

Relationships

  • Heinrich II of Vrey: Alfred is Heinrich’s son.

  • Emilia of Vrey: Emilia is Alfred’s younger sister, and they are very close to each other. Alfred often scolds Emilia for her antics, despite the latter showing no remorse.

  • Leonidas of Hevrion: Leonidas is Alfred’s uncle.
